I am a Ph.D. candidate (combined master's–Ph.D. track) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beijing Normal University, advised by [Prof. Yanlin Luo](https://ai.bnu.edu.cn/xygk/szdw/zgj/9d846b48ef234de0b4821c1710a2c467.htm) and [Assoc. Prof. Hongwen Zhang](https://zhanghongwen.cn/).

I'm interested in **multimodal interaction, embodied intelligence, and immersive learning**. Rather than simply placing knowledge inside a virtual environment, I care more about whether technology can let learners genuinely *participate*—seeing and understanding through avatars and visual interaction, feeling through physical-object tracking and haptic feedback, and using large models to help the system understand the situation and respond naturally.

I hope to keep exploring along this direction, gradually bringing vision, haptics, and intelligent interaction together, and ultimately shaping an **intelligent teacher** that can converse, demonstrate, and collaborate with learners naturally—while studying how this more embodied and interactive way of learning will change the way people understand knowledge.
